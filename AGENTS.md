# Project Instructions & Constraints

## Product Images Preservation Rule (Strict)
- **DO NOT CHANGE PRODUCT IMAGES WHEN PUBLISHING THE WEBSITE.**
- Under no circumstances should product images be modified, replaced, regenerated with AI, deleted, or altered when building, deploying, or publishing the website.
- The product images in `/public/assets/sarees/` and their declarations in `/src/data/products.ts` represent the authentic, boutique-curated photography of AAVIRA BOUTIQUE and must be preserved exactly as they are.
- Ensure all product image files in `/public/assets/sarees/` remain canonical and intact across all environments (development, preview, production build, and export).
