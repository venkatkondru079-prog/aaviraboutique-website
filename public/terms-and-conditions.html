Return & Exchange Terms

1

Return window

You can request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery for online orders, or from the date of purchase for in-store orders.

Requests after this window won't be accepted, other than for manufacturing defects — see point 4.

2

Condition of the item

For an easy, no-fuss return, the item should come back to us:

  • Unused, unwashed, and unaltered
  • With all original tags, Silk Mark labels, and packaging intact
  • Free of perfume, makeup, or other marks

Items with any custom stitching, blouse alterations, or falls/edging done at your request aren't eligible for return.

3

What's not eligible

  • Items bought on clearance or flat-discount sale (30% off or more)
  • Custom-stitched, tailored blouse pieces, or made-to-order bridal ensembles
  • Innerwear, peticoats, and other hygiene-sensitive items
  • Combo or gift sets — these are returned as a full set only
4

Valid reasons for a return

  • Manufacturing defect — a flaw in the fabric, pure zari weaving, or print
  • Wrong item received — incorrect color, style, or product dispatched
  • Damaged in transit — reported within 48 hours, with a photo or video of the unboxing

A change of mind after wearing or trying on the item, or minor colour variations arising from photographic lighting / display screen settings, aren't covered.

5

How to request a return

  • Message us on WhatsApp (+91 93471 59353), email (aavira.bo@gmail.com), or call with your order number
  • Send a photo or short clip of the item, and of the issue if it's a defect or damage
  • Our concierge will confirm approval within 2 business days
  • The item is scheduled for doorstep courier pickup or dropped off at our flagship store
  • After inspection, you will hear back with the final confirmation within 3–5 business days
6

Refunds

Refunds are credited to your original payment method (Bank wire, UPI, or Card) within 7–10 business days of the item passing inspection. Store credit is also available immediately, often with a 5% loyalty bonus added.

Delivery charges are non-refundable, unless the return is because of our error or a genuine manufacturing flaw.
7

Exchanges

We are happy to swap your item for another size, color, or saree weave design, subject to stock availability. If we're out of what you'd like, you can pick something else of equal or higher value, or take a full refund instead. One exchange permitted per order.

8

Shipping costs

If the return is due to our mistake or transit damage, AAVIRA BOUTIQUE covers 100% of the return courier shipping. If it is by customer choice — such as a color or pattern preference — the return shipping cost is borne by the customer.

9

Quality check

Every return is meticulously inspected by our master weavers upon arrival at our atelier. If it does not meet the condition outlined in clause 2, it will be safely returned to you and the return will not be processed.
